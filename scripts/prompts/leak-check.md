The user message is one short policy text from a 2024 U.S. party platform: the Democratic, the Republican or the Libertarian platform.

Say which party's platform proposed it, and which words in the text point to that party.

## The fields

- `party`: D for Democratic, R for Republican or L for Libertarian. Pick one, even when you're unsure.
- `cues`: the words or short phrases in the text that point to that party, copied exactly as they appear. List only words that point to the party. If the policy itself gave the party away and no particular words did, leave the list empty.
